Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 23, 2026

Flight Whisper ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application and website.

1. Information We Use

Flight Whisper is designed to collect as little data as possible.

Location Data
Used to provide navigation, nearby airport search, distance, and bearing features.
Location data is NOT stored or transmitted to our servers.

Microphone / Speech
Used for voice commands.
Speech may be processed using Apple Speech Recognition services.
We do NOT record or store voice audio.

Device & Diagnostic Data
Used to improve performance, stability, and fix bugs.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide app functionality
  • Improve performance and reliability
  • Fix bugs and crashes

3. Data Storage

We do NOT store personal data on our servers. Most processing happens locally on your device.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services such as:

  • Apple Speech Recognition
  • Apple Location Services
  • Mapbox
  • FAA chart and aviation data services
  • Analytics or crash reporting tools

5. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, rent, or share your data.

6. Aviation Disclaimer

Flight Whisper is intended for situational awareness only and is not a certified navigation system. It should not be used as a sole source for flight decisions.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted with a revised effective date.

8. Contact

For questions, contact: fbo@flightwhisper.app
